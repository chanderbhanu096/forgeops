# risk-register

## When to use

Use for projects, releases, migrations, experiments, and operations.

## Workflow

1. Describe each risk as cause, event, and impact.
2. Score likelihood and impact, then assign mitigation and owner.
3. Track triggers and contingency actions for high risks.

## Output contract

A prioritized risk register.
